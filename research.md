## AI-Native Opportunity

- **Proactive AI advisor:** Rather than waiting for an advisor to notice a flag, an AI agent could continuously monitor LMS engagement, grade trajectories, and attendance patterns and initiate a personalised outreach message to the student at the optimal moment, dramatically reducing the human bottleneck in early intervention.
- **Natural language advising dialogue:** Students resist interacting with dashboards; an LLM-powered advising chatbot available 24/7 can answer "what courses should I take next semester?", parse degree audit data, and surface options in plain language — far more actionable than static degree-plan PDFs.
- **Cross-institutional transfer credit intelligence:** AI can parse transfer credit policies from thousands of institutions and match a transfer student's transcripts to degree requirements automatically, a task that currently consumes hundreds of advisor-hours per year.
- **Wellbeing signal detection:** LLMs can be fine-tuned to detect early indicators of mental-health stress in student-written communications (help-desk tickets, advising chat logs) and route sensitive cases to counselling staff with appropriate privacy safeguards.
- **Predictive cohort modelling with causal reasoning:** Existing platforms provide risk scores; an AI-native system can explain *why* a student is at risk and which specific intervention type (tutoring, financial aid, schedule change) has the highest probability of reversing the trajectory based on comparable peer outcomes.
